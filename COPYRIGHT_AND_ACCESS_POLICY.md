# Copyright and Access Policy

## Principle

The Product Leadership Academy is a learning system, not a content archive. It stores original synthesis, analytical notes, limited quotations with proper attribution, and source metadata. It does not reproduce copyrighted works.

## Rules

### Permitted
1. Source metadata (title, author, URL, publication date, etc.).
2. Limited quotations (short excerpts) with exact source locations for analytical purposes.
3. Original synthesis, analysis, and commentary on sources.
4. Links to publicly accessible sources.
5. Personal notes and summaries written by the Academy maintainer.
6. References to publicly available syllabi, course descriptions, and materials.

### Not Permitted
1. Reproduction of entire books, articles, courses, or substantial portions thereof.
2. Copying proprietary course materials, slide decks, or workbooks.
3. Scraping or archiving private community content.
4. Circumventing paywalls or access controls.
5. Storing downloaded videos, podcasts, or other media files.
6. Republishing copyrighted content under the Academy's name.
7. Creating derivative works that substitute for the original.

### Grey Areas — Err on the Side of Caution
1. Course syllabi: Reference public descriptions; do not reproduce full detailed syllabi.
2. Conference talks: Reference with timestamps; do not reproduce full transcripts unless creator-provided.
3. Company documentation: Reference public docs; do not reproduce internal or confidential materials.
4. Practitioner communities: Reference that a discussion exists; do not reproduce threads.
5. YouTube content: Link with timestamps; use ASR-derived excerpts only with appropriate labelling.

## Access Statuses

- `OPEN_ACCESS` — Freely and legally accessible
- `REGISTRATION_REQUIRED` — Free but requires account creation
- `PAID_ACCESS` — Requires purchase or subscription
- `ACCESS_REQUIRED` — Requires credentials not currently held
- `RESTRICTED` — Not publicly accessible (internal, confidential)
- `UNKNOWN` — Access status not verified

## Attribution

All sources must be attributed with:
- Author or creator name
- Title of work
- Publication or recording date
- URL or identifier
- Page, section, timestamp, or other exact location for specific claims

## Fair Use

Where limited quotations are used, they are justified by:
1. Analytical or educational purpose
2. Small proportion of the original work
3. No market substitution for the original
4. Proper attribution and source location

## Incident Reporting

If a copyright concern is identified, the material must be:
1. Flagged in the contradiction register
2. Reviewed against this policy
3. Removed or replaced if in violation
4. Recorded in the changelog

## Review

This policy must be reviewed annually or when a new source category is added.
