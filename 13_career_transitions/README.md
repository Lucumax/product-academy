# Module 13: Career Transitions

**Status:** v0.2.0 — New module
**Audience:** Senior PM through CPO; also PMs considering a transition into or out of product
**Prerequisites:** Modules 00–01 (Orientation, Core Doctrine)

---

## Purpose

The Academy teaches product leadership capability. This module covers what happens when that capability must be *packaged, evidenced, and sold* — to a hiring panel, an internal promotion committee, or a market that is evaluating you as a candidate.

It has three parts:

1. **Landing a Product Role** — What the hiring process actually rewards at each seniority level, and how to prepare for it honestly. This is not interview tactics. It is the application of Academy doctrine (evidence over authority, capability over credential) to your own candidacy.
2. **Building a Credibility Binder** — The durable, public evidence of your product judgment: writing, portfolios, artifacts, and demonstrable decision quality. A credibility binder is the anti-resume — it shows what you *do*, not what you claim.
3. **Emerging Product Roles** — How the role is evolving (AI product management, platform, developer-adjacent, data) and what that means for capability development and positioning.

The through-line is the same as the rest of the Academy: **the quality of the evidence matters more than the strength of the assertion.** A hiring process that cannot distinguish capability from performance is a flawed evidence system — and the candidate who understands that can navigate it more honestly and more effectively.

## What This Module Is NOT

- **NOT interview tactics or "behavioral answer" templates.** The Academy rejects performative competence. Role-specific question banks belong elsewhere.
- **NOT a job board or recruiter service.** No listings, no outreach scripts, no cold-email spam.
- **NOT a credentialing program.** The Academy does not certify, and completing this module grants no qualification.
- **NOT a replacement for the capability model.** This module helps you *evidence* capability; it does not define capability. The capability model (00_orientation/CAPABILITY_MODEL.md) is the standard.

## Recommended Reading Order

1. **LANDING_A_PRODUCT_ROLE.md** — Start here if you are preparing for a search or promotion process. It maps the process to seniority and tells you what is being tested at each level.
2. **CREDIBILITY_BINDER.md** — Build your evidence artifact before you need it. A binder built during a search is reactive; a binder built continuously is a capability amplifier.
3. **EMERGING_PRODUCT_ROLES.md** — Read this when positioning for the future, choosing where to deepen, or evaluating whether your current specialization is a durable bet.

## Epistemic Labeling

Like the handbook, this module labels claims:

- **[E] Evidence** — empirically documented outcome
- **[P] Practitioner doctrine** — widely held but unverified
- **[I] Inference** — reasonable but unverified reasoning
- **[D] Open debate** — practitioners disagree
- **[R] Practical recommendation** — what to do given what is known

Career guidance is dominated by practitioner doctrine and inference. The module is explicit about which claims are evidence and which are belief. A hiring process that rewards the wrong thing is evidence of a flawed process, not of capability — and the module says so plainly.
