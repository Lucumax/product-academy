# Channel 2 — Direct PM Reviewer Outreach

Status: **SPEC PREPARED — NO NAMES INVENTED, NOTHING SENT.** Do not send until Walter
approves the recipient list and the exact message.

The ask is deliberately small: evaluate three blinded examples, report failures honestly, no
obligation to share or star.

## Selection criteria (build the list from these)

For each candidate, all of the following:

1. Experienced PM or product leader (5+ years, or Senior/Principal/GPM/Director/VP/founder).
2. Public interest in AI-assisted product work (they have written, posted, spoken, or built
   about it) — so the request is relevant, not cold.
3. Known or evident ability to criticize rather than merely promote (their public history
   shows critical takes, not hype-only).
4. Varied seniority and product model across the list (Senior PM, Principal/GPM,
   Director/VP, founder, AI PM; and across B2B / consumer / marketplace / developer tool /
   AI product).
5. No conflict of interest that would require them to endorse anything (they are not paid,
   employed, or obligated by us).

## List-building spec (15–20 people)

For each candidate record:

- Name / handle (verified — from their own profile or public writing, not guessed).
- Role and company (as publicly stated).
- Why they fit: which criteria (1–5) they meet, with a link or reference for their AI-PM
  interest and their critical-taking history.
- Contact method (their public channel: email from their site, LinkedIn, X, a form).
- Product model and seniority (to balance the cohort).

Deliverable: a table of 15–20 verified candidates matching the mix. **No fabricated names.
Every name must be verifiable before any message is sent.**

## Response templates

### Genuine question

> Good question. [Answer; e.g. about how the four methods were generated, how blinding works,
> or what the scenarios cover.] The analysis plan is precommitted here: [link to
> ANALYSIS_PLAN.md]. Happy to clarify anything else.

### Skeptical criticism

> That's a fair criticism, and it's the kind I need. [If method-level:] I'll note it and
> address it in the write-up — the study's limits are as much a finding as the scores. [If a
> specific output failed:] I'll record the failure with your notes. Publishing honest
> failures is the point.

### Installation problem

> Thanks for telling me. Can you share the command you ran, your agent and version, and the
> error? I'll reproduce it, fix it, and add it to the installation matrix — install failures
> are the highest-value feedback I get.

### Competing resource recommendation

> Thanks — I'll look at [resource]. If it does this better with less friction, that's exactly
> what the evaluation is for, and I'd rather know than not.
