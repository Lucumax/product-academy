# Positioning Decision

Date: 2026-08-02.

## Selected category

**AI-agent skills for product management** (a category with two verified, high-traffic
competitors: `deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills` 6,199★, `RefoundAI/lenny-skills` 1,212★).
This is the search intent a working PM actually has ("product management skills Claude",
"PM skills Codex", "AI skills for product managers"). Choosing this category wins visibility;
the differentiator (below) wins the visitor.

## Primary audience

- Product managers (Senior → Principal → Group PM) who work with AI agents (Claude Code,
  Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, ChatGPT).
- Product leaders and Directors/VPs who make high-stakes calls (GO/NO-GO, PMF, launch).
- Founders doing product work with an agent.
- AI product managers (the `check-ai-evaluation-contract` skill is a rare differentiator).

## Primary job

**Make a defensible product decision** — not "write a memo." The skills produce a decision
artifact (problem frame, weighted discovery synthesis, ranked backlog, experiment charter,
stakeholder map, GO/NO-GO verdict) with a next action.

## Primary differentiator

**Evidence-backed decisions.** Competing packs supply frameworks and templates; this pack
grades and uses evidence — a 15-type taxonomy in which internal product evidence
(experiments, cohorts, analytics, interviews, support, win/loss) is first-class and never
auto-outranked by published research. Every skill separates evidence from assumption from
inference, and runs fast (reversible decisions) or full (one-way doors). This is the Academy's
evidence/judgment layer turned executable.

## Claims that must NOT be made yet

- "9/10" or any rating of the product.
- "Best / ultimate / industry standard."
- Any star, install, adoption, or benchmark number (all are zero or unverified).
- That skills.sh indexing, Search Console submission, or any directory listing has occurred.
- That any person or organization endorses or will promote the repository.

## Reversal conditions

Change this positioning if any of the following becomes true:

- Verified search data shows "AI agent skills" is not the intent PMs use to find this
  category (e.g. "product management frameworks" dominates) — then broaden to
  "product management frameworks, executable by AI agents."
- The evidence-backed differentiator fails comparative evaluation (Phase 8/11) — then lead
  with coverage (job-based breadth) instead.
- A competitor owns the exact phrase and the term is crowded beyond practical ranking — then
  differentiate on the sub-term ("evidence-based product decisions").
- The audience data (stars, installs, search) shows founders/leaders, not IC PMs, are the
  actual users — then re-target the hero and keywords accordingly.
