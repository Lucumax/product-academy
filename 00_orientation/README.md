# Product Leadership Academy — Orientation

Welcome to the Product Leadership Academy. This is not a course catalog, a certification program, or a collection of best practices. It is a deliberately structured, opinionated curriculum for developing elite product leadership capability — from Senior PM through CPO.

## What This Academy Is

A **developmental curriculum** built on the premise that product leadership is a discipline with identifiable capabilities, learnable skills, and assessable standards. The Academy provides:

1. **A comprehensive capability model** that defines what product leaders at every level must actually be able to do — not what credentials they hold or frameworks they know
2. **Structured learning paths** for each role level, organized around the capabilities that distinguish great from adequate at that level
3. **Deep content modules** covering product archetypes, business models, industry overlays, AI product management, and the hardest judgment calls in the discipline
4. **Practice and assessment tools** including a simulator, personal lab, and quality gates for honest self-assessment and organizational evaluation

## What This Academy Is Not

- **Not a framework catalog.** You will not find exhaustive taxonomies of product frameworks. Frameworks are tools; this Academy develops the judgment to know which tool to use when — and when to use none.
- **Not a certification mill.** There is no badge, no certificate, no letters to add to your title. The only certification that matters is demonstrable capability in your work.
- **Not a collection of case studies with obvious answers.** The Academy's cases are designed to develop judgment under ambiguity, not to illustrate tidy principles.
- **Not a substitute for experience.** The Academy accelerates learning from experience; it does not replace it. If you are not currently practicing product management in some form, much of this material will be abstract.

## Core Principles

1. **Capability over Credential.** What you can do matters more than where you've worked, what you've studied, or who you know. The Academy's capability model is the authoritative standard. Everything else serves it.

2. **Outcomes over Output.** The Academy measures success by whether your organization creates more value through your leadership — not by how many frameworks you can name, how polished your PRDs are, or how well you perform in interviews.

3. **Judgment over Process.** Process is a tool, not a product. The Academy develops the judgment to know when process helps, when it hurts, and when it's being used to avoid hard decisions.

4. **Depth over Breadth.** It is better to develop genuine excellence in three capabilities than superficial familiarity with twenty. The Academy's learning paths are designed for depth.

5. **Honesty over Comfort.** Self-assessment is only useful if it's accurate. The Academy's quality gates, false positive patterns, and anti-pattern catalog exist to puncture comfortable self-deceptions — because comfortable self-deception is the primary obstacle to development.

6. **Practice over Consumption.** Reading about product leadership is not practicing product leadership. Every module includes practice methods. The simulator and personal lab exist to create practice opportunities that are hard to find in daily work.

## How to Start

### If you have 10 minutes
Read [NAVIGATION_GUIDE.md](NAVIGATION_GUIDE.md). It will direct you to the right starting point.

### If you have 60 minutes
Read the [CAPABILITY_MODEL.md](CAPABILITY_MODEL.md). Self-assess honestly on all capabilities at your current level. Identify your three biggest gaps.

### If you have a day
Read the capability model, self-assess, then follow your role's learning path through the first module. Complete the practice exercises.

### If you're evaluating this for your organization
Start with the capability model. It is the architecture on which everything else depends. Then review [QUALITY_GATES.md](../QUALITY_GATES.md) for how the Academy handles assessment. Then review the learning path for the level you're most concerned about developing.

## Version and Updates

The Academy is a living curriculum. Capabilities evolve as the discipline evolves. Content is updated based on contribution from practitioners, research from the Academy's research function, and lessons from the simulator.

If you contribute a case, a contradiction, or a capability refinement that improves the model, that contribution becomes part of the Academy with attribution. See [SOURCE_POLICY.md](../SOURCE_POLICY.md) for details on sourcing and attribution.
