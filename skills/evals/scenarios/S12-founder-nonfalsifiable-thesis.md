# Scenario 12 — Pre-Revenue Product With a Non-Falsifiable Thesis

**Domain:** Early-stage. **Type:** weak evidence, early-stage.

## Context

A founder is raising for an AI "executive briefing" tool. The pitch: "Every exec will want
AI-prepared briefings." No segment, no problem statement beyond "execs are busy," no mechanism
beyond "AI," and the evidence is three friendly pilot demos. An investor asks what would
disprove it. The founder says "we'll iterate until it works."

## Inputs available (imperfect)

- The pitch verbatim; three pilot demo sessions (friendly, founder-moderated).
- No interviews with actual executives about the briefing workflow.
- No willingness-to-pay; no cohort; no falsification condition.
- The founder is committed to the direction.

## Skills applied and run record

**1. `frame-product-problem` (full).** Problem: "execs are busy" (not a job-to-be-done;
unverifiable). Segment: "every exec" (unfalsifiable). Outcome: none measurable. Solution: "AI
briefings" (means, not a named mechanism). Unsupported assumptions: every element. Verdict:
`PARTIALLY-FRAMED` at best — the segment and outcome slots cannot be filled. Next action:
3–5 discovery interviews with execs on the *briefing workflow* (what they read, why, what
they skip) before any pitch.

**2. `pressure-test-product-thesis` (full).** Slots: segment vague, problem unstated in
customer terms, outcome non-measurable. Falsification condition: none — "we'll iterate" is the
exact non-answer the skill targets. Evidence: presentation-derived (friendly pilot demos),
no discovery base. Verdict: `UNDERSPECIFIED`, and once prompted to a falsification condition,
`BELIEF-PRESENTED-AS-THESIS` (the team refuses to commit to a test that can fail). Confidence
High. Next action: a pre-committed falsification test (e.g. "of 10 execs shown the briefing
weekly for 4 weeks, fewer than 4 keep reading it → the value assumption is wrong").

**3. `audit-decision-evidence` (fast).** Claims: (a) demand exists — evidence: three friendly
pilot demos (E5/E15-grade, founder-moderated, selection-biased) → UNDER-SUPPORTED; (b) execs
will pay — NO-EVIDENCE. Decision verdict: NO-GO on committing significant build/raise spend to
this thesis as stated.

**4. `assess-product-market-fit-health` (fast, pre-revenue archetype).** Signal proxies: three
pilots (anecdote), no willingness-to-pay, no interviews. Verdict: `UNMEASURED` — the pilot
demos are not a signal battery. The archetype battery names what to stand up.

## Verdict produced

UNDERSPECIFIED/BELIEF-PRESENTED-AS-THESIS; NO-GO on committing to the thesis as stated;
UNMEASURED on any PMF signal. The honest advice: run 5 discovery interviews and write a
falsification test before raising. The founder's "we'll iterate" is recorded as the refusal
that makes the verdict.
