# Personal Lab — Applying Academy Doctrine to Walter's Portfolio

## What This Is

The Personal Lab is where the Product Leadership Academy doctrine gets applied to Walter's actual portfolio initiatives, not hypothetical scenarios. Every claim is labeled with its evidence status to maintain intellectual honesty about what is known versus what is inferred.

## How It Works

### Evidence Labels

Every claim in the application documents carries one of these labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Local-source fact** | Verifiable from Walter's own repositories, data, or direct experience |
| **External fact** | Verifiable from public sources, research, or third-party evidence |
| **Inference** | Logical conclusion drawn from available evidence but not directly observed |
| **Recommendation** | What should be done based on evidence and inference |
| **Unknown** | Genuinely uncertain; requires investigation before acting |

### Application Structure

Each initiative application follows the same structure, forcing comprehensive analysis:

1. **Product or initiative** — What it is
2. **Product archetype** — Which Academy archetype(s) it maps to
3. **Industry** — Industry context
4. **Organizational stage** — Where it is in the lifecycle
5. **Current user** — Who uses it
6. **Buyer** — Who pays
7. **Problem thesis** — What problem it solves (or claims to)
8. **Evidence status** — What we actually know
9. **Product-leadership questions** — Questions raised by Academy doctrine
10. **Principal-level trade-offs** — Non-obvious tensions
11. **Risks** — What could go wrong
12. **Missing validation** — What hasn't been tested
13. **Most useful Academy doctrine** — Which principles, cases, frameworks are most relevant
14. **Cheapest decisive test** — Minimum to advance or kill
15. **What would reverse the current view** — Reversal conditions

### Using the Lab

1. Review the Portfolio Overview first for cross-cutting patterns
2. Dive into individual applications for initiative-specific analysis
3. Use evidence labels to decide what needs more investigation
4. Revisit quarterly — the evidence changes, the applications should too
5. The lab is self-directed. Quality of analysis is its own reward.

### Connection to Academy Doctrine

The personal lab references doctrine from across the Academy:
- Core principles (PRN-0001 through PRN-0012) from Track 01
- Principal+ capabilities from Track 02
- Product archetypes from Track 04
- AI product management from Track 05
- Industry overlays from Track 06
- Cases from Track 07
- Contradictions from Track 08

### Honesty About Gaps

The lab is designed to surface what we don't know. Applications that reveal large evidence gaps are successful — they identify where investigation is needed. The worst outcome is a confident application built on weak evidence.

*The Personal Lab is an ongoing practice, not a one-time artifact. It should be revised as evidence accumulates and doctrine is refined.*
