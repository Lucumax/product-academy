# Academy Scope

## What the Academy Is

The Product Leadership Academy is an evidence-backed education system for product leaders from Senior PM through CPO. It provides:

1. **Reference material** — Doctrine, cases, frameworks, and tools for real product decisions.
2. **Practice environment** — Scenarios, simulations, and scored assessments for deliberate practice.
3. **Personal application** — Templates and maps for applying doctrine to Walter's real portfolio.
4. **Integration layer** — Connection to Product Forge for applying learning to real initiatives.

## What the Academy Is Not

The Academy is not an execution operating system. It does not replace:

- **Product Forge** — Evidence-to-backlog execution
- **VSH** — Verified software implementation
- **Ops Hub** — Portfolio-level prioritization
- **Hermes** — Bounded campaign orchestration

## Scope Boundaries

### In Scope
- Product leadership doctrine (Senior PM through CPO)
- Product archetypes and their implications
- Industry-specific constraints and adaptations
- AI product management
- Principal+ decision-making
- Practice and assessment
- Source provenance and evidence quality
- Career navigation — landing product roles by seniority, building a credibility binder, and understanding emerging product roles (bounded; see Track 13)
- Personal application to Walter's portfolio

### Out of Scope
- Software engineering instruction
- General management training
- Personalized career coaching or representation — the Academy provides doctrine and playbooks, not individual coaching, application execution, or offer negotiation on your behalf
- Venture capital or fundraising
- Sales or marketing operations
- People management fundamentals (hiring, firing, performance reviews)
- Certification or credentialing
- Course platform or LMS

## Depth Priorities

When breadth conflicts with depth, prioritize:
1. Repository and source integrity
2. Ecosystem boundaries
3. Principal+ capability model
4. Pilot curriculum (problem selection and resource allocation)
5. AI PM module
6. Regulated-industry overlays
7. Cases and simulations
8. Broader corpus coverage
