## Uncorroborated claims (single-source)

These rest on one source and should be treated as weaker doctrine:

- **CLM-0003** — Single-threaded leadership (one leader, one team, one mission) reduces coordination overhead and improves outcomes
- **CLM-0004** — The transition from individual contributor to manager is one of the hardest career shifts, requiring deliberate skill development
- **CLM-0005** — Good strategy has a kernel: diagnosis of the challenge, guiding policy for addressing it, and coherent actions to execute
- **CLM-0007** — The 'working backwards' approach (starting from customer experience and working backwards to technology) produces better products
- **CLM-0009** — Build-Measure-Learn cycles with MVPs produce better validated learning than upfront specification
- **CLM-0010** — Opportunity Solution Trees provide structured visual framework for connecting outcomes to opportunities to solutions to experiments
- **CLM-0013** — In crisis (wartime), leadership must make decisions with incomplete information and live with consequences; standard management frameworks don't apply
- **CLM-0015** — Design sprints (5-day time-boxed process) can compress months of debate and prototyping into a validated direction
- **CLM-0017** — A startup is a company designed to grow fast; growth is definitional, not aspirational
- **CLM-0018** — LLMs have a 'jagged frontier' of capability — they excel at some tasks in a domain and fail at apparently similar tasks
- **CLM-0019** — Model cards (structured transparency documentation) increase accountability and enable downstream users to make informed decisions about ML model use
- **CLM-0021** — Technology products face a 'chasm' between early adopters and mainstream market; crossing it requires a focused beachhead strategy
- **CLM-0022** — Incumbent firms fail when they listen to their best customers and ignore disruptive technologies that initially serve different markets
- **CLM-0024** — Premature scaling — investing heavily in marketing and distribution before validating product-market fit — amplifies failure rather than accelerating success
- **CLM-0026** — Software as a Medical Device requires risk-based classification, clinical evaluation, and quality management systems that differ fundamentally from consumer software development
- **CLM-0027** — Privacy regulations (GDPR) require data protection by design and default, creating architectural constraints on product development that must be considered from inception
- **CLM-0029** — Good design makes affordances visible, provides clear feedback, and uses conceptual models that match users' mental models
- **CLM-0032** — The 'Spotify model' (squads, tribes, chapters, guilds) was aspirational and has been widely misunderstood as a fixed blueprint rather than a point-in-time description
- **CLM-0033** — Products should do things that don't scale in early stages to gain initial traction and learn
- **CLM-0034** — Subscriptions create better incentives for product quality than advertising because the customer is also the user
